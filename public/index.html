After Care

  • Facials: Avoid direct sun for 24 hours, moisturize, and wear SPF daily.
  • Lash Extensions: Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours, avoid oil-based products, and brush daily.
  • Waxing: Skip hot showers, saunas, or swimming for 24 hours; moisturize with a gentle lotion.
  • Chemical Peels: Mild redness or peeling may occur for 2–3 days; avoid exfoliating products until healed.
