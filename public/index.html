About Me

Hello there, I’m Lucy, the lash artist behind Spellbound by Lucy. I’ve always been drawn to the idea of transformation — the way a small change can completely shift how we feel about ourselves. That’s what inspired me to create a space where anyone can feel comfortable expressing and celebrating their authentic selves. With two years of experience in the beauty industry, I specialize in lash extensions and waxing with an eye for detail and a gentle touch. My goal isn’t just to give you amazing results, but to make sure you feel cared for as you walk in and confident as you walk out. Spellbound by Lucy was created to capture that celestial, timeless beauty we all have within us. Whatever you come in for, I promise to make your experience relaxing, safe, and tailored to you. I can’t wait to meet you and help you feel your most radiant self!

After Care

  • Facials: Avoid direct sun for 24 hours, moisturize, and wear SPF daily.
  • Lash Extensions: Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours, avoid oil-based products, and brush daily.
  • Waxing: Skip hot showers, saunas, or swimming for 24 hours; moisturize with a gentle lotion.
  • Chemical Peels: Mild redness or peeling may occur for 2–3 days; avoid exfoliating products until healed.
